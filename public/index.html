How the test line works

  1. Your browser captures 16 kHz PCM and streams it over WebSocket to the call engine.
  2. VAD detects speech, STT transcribes, the LLM streams a reply, TTS is chunked into 20 ms frames.
  3. Speaking over the agent triggers barge-in: playback and generation stop immediately.
